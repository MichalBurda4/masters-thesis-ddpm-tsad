\section{Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item End-to-end DDPM pipeline for multivariate TSAD with partial diffusion scoring.
  \item Systematic comparison with strong reconstruction baselines.
  \item Ablation of diffusion depth $k$ and attribution maps.
\end{itemize}

\section{Future work}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Score-based SDE models as an alternative diffusion formulation.
  \item Broader multi-machine SMD evaluation and additional labeled benchmarks.
  \item Improved thresholding and calibration for precision--recall trade-offs.
  \item Faster inference (DDIM / distillation) for online monitoring.
\end{itemize}
